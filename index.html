Our Vision & Mission

BLACK HOXS is an independent digital production and technology company founded and owned by Aneek Mallick, professionally known by the alias Andres. Established with a long-term vision to operate as a parent organization, BLACK HOXS develops, owns, manages, and operates multiple digital brands, web platforms, technology products, and creative ventures under one unified ecosystem.

Just as major holding companies manage distinct subsidiaries, BLACK HOXS provides the overarching infrastructure, engineering rigor, and brand identity that allows each independent project to scale globally. We are not merely a web development agency; we are a continuous incubator of digital innovation. In practice, we are a software development house: we plan, design, engineer and launch websites, web applications, AI and data-driven products, and digital brands for our own ecosystem and for clients.
